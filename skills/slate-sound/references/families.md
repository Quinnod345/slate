# Families: one sound per kind of interaction

## One family per kind, each its own material

The warm starting recipe maps touch to wood, paper to paper, marks to graphite and ink, moves to air, chapter swells to
tuned air or a pad, a character to its motif, and a big landing to a deep, hollow sound. Consistent families help the
viewer learn that language. The current palette and authorized feedback can choose other materials, shared sounds or
deliberate repetition; do not treat this earlier mapping as universal.

- **Be strong-willed about what each beat is.** The builders tag every beat with its kind and size (slate-build); a beat
  with the wrong kind gets the wrong sound.
- **The starter separates kinds.** Its tap and snap have different sounds; the chosen palette's rules decide whether
  sharing serves another treatment.
- **Unknown kinds are reported, never silently dropped.**

## Variation that sounds played, not random

The same sample repeated reads as cheap: one film's first mix put 483 cues on 48 distinct sounds, with runs of up to
eleven identical hits. The fix was designed variation:
- variations within a family differ the way real playing does: pitch within the key's pentatonic, the material's colour,
  the strike, the timing of a second contact;
- the starter avoids consecutive identical variations; the configured repeat policy can permit intentional repetition;
- **bursts are gestures**: a list writing on is one writing gesture, a run of dots is one run walking up the scale, not
  twelve identical clicks;
- density stays readable (no more than about eight transients in half a second).

## Sizes

`s` a small detail, `m` normal, `l` a moment. Size scales the sound's weight, not just its volume: a large land is a
deeper knock, not a louder small one.

## Impacts

- The starting recipe uses **one impact per act** and a different file for each. The brief can choose several impacts
  or a recurring hit; declare their timing and use the chosen palette's repeat policy.
- A big impact gets a **tempo echo** on the score's beat (a dotted eighth or a quarter note), each repeat about 6.5 dB
  softer and darker, panned to the other side.
- Land it at the end of a sentence, so it plays in the voice's pause, and let music and hit land together.
- Before it, a held breath: the effects fall silent and the music stops dead.

## A character

A brand character's sounds are its motif (small felt notes, one per part of the mark, or a short phrase), a small wood
tick for pointing, and soft pads. One event per note, each on its own contact frame, and when two of its sounds land on
the same frame only one plays. The score yields to a pad under the character's motif, so its notes carry the melody.
