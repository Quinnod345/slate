# The review checklist

What to look for, shot by shot and across the film. The automated gates cover what they can; this is what a person
checks on the contact sheets, the strips and the preview (at full speed and slower, with sound). After the automated
per-shot checks, look at the contact sheets yourself, and look hard: hierarchy, emptiness, legibility, the look, sync.

## Every shot

**Motion**
- [ ] No pops, jumps or twisting; nothing appears from nothing or vanishes in one frame.
- [ ] No overshoot or bounce, except the brand's named settle.
- [ ] Every move has a reason: a word or an idea. One idea per beat.
- [ ] Big moves start and end at rest; nothing smears on one frame and snaps sharp on the next.
- [ ] Characters are still between gestures and calm at the act's ends.

**Image**
- [ ] No aliasing, crawling thin lines, shimmering patterns or moire (check at the smallest delivery size).
- [ ] Nothing clipped that shouldn't be; close-ups don't crop text mid-line.
- [ ] Text is legible: size, contrast, and held long enough.
- [ ] One clear focal object; nothing empty that should be composed.

**Joins**
- [ ] Hand-offs across shot and act joins are carries, with no pop.
- [ ] Contract frames match; nothing decorative is still fading at the cut.

**Footage**
- [ ] No interpolation artifacts on generated plates or captures (haze, thin features, edges).
- [ ] Nothing generated reads as AI.

**Content**
- [ ] No banned words; the on-screen text matches the script.
- [ ] Every product element is true to the product; sample data is labelled.

## The whole film

- [ ] The look is the brand's (against its approved look reference), from the first frame to the last.
- [ ] Each act's signature moment lands, and meets the visionary bar.
- [ ] The voice is always understood; every brand word is said in full.
- [ ] Sound is locked to the picture: every visible beat has its sound within a frame; nothing sounds that the eye
      doesn't follow.
- [ ] The mix is balanced: small accents audible, textures and swells under the voice, no effect louder than it.
- [ ] Planned silences are there (the held breath, the ending on the last frame).
- [ ] Captions are right, and inside the platforms' safe zones.
- [ ] The call to action is on the end card in text as well as in the voice.

## Before a sitting

- [ ] You've watched the phone cut end to end yourself, with sound.
- [ ] Every open REVIEW item is either fixed or waiting for the owner, with its evidence.
- [ ] The packet says what changed and asks only what the owner must decide.
