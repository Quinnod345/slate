# Review packets

A review packet is what the owner sees of a version. `slate review` builds it (slate-finish); this is what goes in it
and how to review what's there.

## In every packet

- **The phone cut**, with burned-in timecode and shot id, so every note maps to a shot.
- **A 0.5-second contact sheet** of the whole film, labelled with shot and time.
- **What changed** since the last version, in plain words.
- **The open questions**, each one decision, asked as concretely as possible.
- **The gates' status** for what changed (only PASS counts; anything else is named).

## When a packet carries more

| Material | For |
|---|---|
| Voice-only previews | the voice shortlist, or a re-voice |
| Casting reels and tag A/B clips, level-matched | choosing a voice or a direction by ear |
| Per-shot half-resolution previews with sound | hero moments |
| Side-by-side comparisons | two takes, before and after a fix, code against a generated bridge |
| Gesture sheets | a brand character's gestures |
| A sound proof with a labelled spectrogram | sound decisions |
| Generated takes on a contact sheet | picking a take |

## Reviewing a packet (before it's sent)

- **Contact sheets**: review representative frames the way the references were studied: composition, hierarchy, the
  brand's look, legibility, emptiness.
- **Gesture sheets**: every gesture at its smallest and largest size, at full and quarter speed, with sound. Nothing
  shimmers, pops or strobes, and every note lands on something visible.
- **Hero moments**: at full speed and slower, with sound, against the visionary bar.
- **The phone cut**: end to end, with sound, on a small screen if you can: what reads on a laptop can vanish on a phone.

## When the owner answers

Log every note verbatim, numbered, in the tracker; record every approval against its exact file; and turn notes on
approved work into a checklist of changes to that exact artifact (perfect, don't replace).
