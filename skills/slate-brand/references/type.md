# Type in the brand's films

## Roles

The pack names type roles (hero, headline, body, label, numeral, mono…) and maps each to a family and weight. The kit's
type scale sets sizes as fractions of the frame's short side, so type holds across aspects; a brand can override a role's
size.

## Rules

- **When the guidelines and the shipped product disagree on type weights, the film follows the product.** A weight the
  guidelines list but the product never ships stays out.
- **Keep the product's hierarchy**: an eyebrow in the product stays an eyebrow in the film, not a big heading.
- **Tabular figures** on every number that changes, as good products do.
- **Body text at least 28 px at a 1080-line frame**; hero lines are short (six words or fewer) and at most two per act
  write on per character.
- **Film type and product copy are separate layers**: the script's line is set in film type beside the product's own
  copy, never rewritten into it.

## Legibility

Text contrast comes from the brand's colour pairs: at least 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text. The kit derives
missing roles with contrast guaranteed; `slate qa access` checks the pack's pairs. Over footage, a soft brand-colour grade
behind the type, never a box.
