# Demo Studio's taste

Demo Studio is invented for Slate's demo. This skill shows where a brand's own taste lives: inside its pack, next to
its brand.json, never in Slate's shared skills. A real brand's taste skill is written during onboarding from its own
design language and its owner's notes.

## Look

- Warm stone pages, white cards, one clay accent. Clay carries meaning (the line, the accent, the mark's keystone), so
  a frame has at most one clay thing competing for the eye.
- Public Sans at two weights, tabular figures on every number.
- One clear focal object per frame, with generous space around it.

## Motion

- Every visible move uses a named ease from brand.json (arrive, carry, switch, depart, make-room, settle).
- Nothing bounces and nothing scales from zero. Exits run three quarters of their entrance.
- The keystone build is the signature: once per film, never as decoration.

## Sound

- Soft pads, small felt notes, hollow wood clicks and knocks: one deliberate sound per kind of interaction.
- Never a chime, a bell or a pitched riser. Every sound lands within a frame of its beat.

## Words

- Say only what the approved claims say. Sample numbers are always labelled as sample data.
